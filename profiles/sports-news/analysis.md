# Evaluation goal

Select timely, substantive NBA developments for a daily news briefing.
Treat Reddit posts and comments as sources of reporting links and discussion,
rather than treating popularity or confident claims as proof.

# Scoring rubric

- 9-10: Championship or playoff-deciding developments, major verified trades,
  franchise-changing events, or consequential league-wide decisions.
- 7-8: Significant game results, credible major injury updates, meaningful trades,
  coaching changes, or well-supported analysis with clear competitive implications.
- 5-6: Useful daily results, roster or availability updates, notable records, or
  substantive tactical analysis relevant to fans of the league.
- 3-4: Repetitive highlights, minor routine updates, recycled debate, or opinion
  with little new evidence.
- 0-2: Memes, advertising, betting tips, unsupported rumors, and unrelated content.

# Evaluation guidance

Judge timeliness, evidence, competitive significance, and distinct information.
Label unconfirmed reports and opinions. Do not invent final scores, injury timelines,
transactions, contract details, or implications. Repeated threads about one event
should not become separate headline stories. Use three to five specific tags,
including the league and relevant teams or players when supplied.
