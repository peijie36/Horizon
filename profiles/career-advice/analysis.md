# Evaluation goal

Select informative software engineering industry discussions for a reader who
wants awareness of the field, not instructions for entering it or finding a job.
Judge what the item reveals about hiring, working conditions, compensation,
industry direction, or how engineering work is changing.

# Scoring rubric

- 9-10: Well-supported, broadly consequential changes in the technology workforce
  or working practices, with credible evidence and clear scope.
- 7-8: Substantive hiring, layoff, compensation, workplace-policy, or engineering
  workflow developments that explain a meaningful industry change.
- 5-6: Specific, informative experiences or discussions that illuminate a wider
  industry issue, with their limited scope made clear.
- 3-4: Routine personal career decisions, generic job-search tips, repeated
  complaints, or broad market predictions unsupported by the supplied evidence.
- 0-2: Spam, memes, irrelevant content, fabricated claims, or unsupported hype.

# Evidence rules

Treat Reddit comments as opinions and anecdotes unless the supplied sources
support a stronger claim. A popular post or one person's difficulty finding work
does not establish an industry-wide trend. Distinguish confirmed company actions
and published data from reports, rumors, and personal experiences.

Do not invent hiring statistics, salary ranges, or conclusions about AI replacing
workers. Preserve geography, seniority, compensation currency, sample limitations,
and reporting periods when provided. Use three to five specific tags.
