# Software engineering industry awareness

Use this profile for a reader who wants to follow the software engineering
industry without actively pursuing a CS career or looking for a job.

Prioritize hiring and layoff patterns, compensation trends, junior and senior
role demand, offshoring, workplace culture, return-to-office policies, and changes
in how engineering teams work, including the effects of AI tools.

Include specific personal experiences when they illuminate a broader industry
issue. Clearly distinguish an individual's experience from evidence of a trend.
Exclude routine resume reviews, interview drills, study plans, personal offer
comparisons, and generic job-search advice unless they reveal a concrete wider
development. Product releases belong in the technology news profile unless the
main story concerns their effect on engineering work. Exclude spam and memes.
